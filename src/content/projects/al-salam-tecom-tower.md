The refurbishment runs the full height of the building, so the detail had to repeat twenty-three times and still read as one scheme. Lift lobbies are lined in pale stone panels with a continuous cove wash carried around the ceiling perimeter, terminating on a framed artwork set into green marble. The ground-floor lobby takes the same palette at scale — a channel-tufted banquette run on a raised plinth, the stone panel wall behind it, and a green marble feature wall lit by glass globe pendants.

Scope covered the fitout and the MEP works across every public area of the tower.
