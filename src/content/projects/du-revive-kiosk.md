This is a programme rather than a project. The same unit had to land in malls, lobbies and landscaped public areas across Dubai and Abu Dhabi, on sites that were never the same size or serviced the same way, and still read as one brand at every stop.

That put the work in the joinery shop rather than on site. The counter islands are built and dry-fitted complete — oak veneer carcass, white solid-surface top, cable routes and locks already in — then broken down, trucked, and reassembled in a single overnight possession. The illuminated totem carrying the du mark is a fixed component of the set, so the logo sits at the same height above the counter in every location.

The glazed walk-in cabins take the same interior kit inside a framed glass enclosure for sites with no host structure to build against, so a kiosk can be placed on open paving and still offer a seated, air-conditioned position.

Complete fitout on each unit — joinery, solid surface, signage and lighting, power and data, and the installation itself.
