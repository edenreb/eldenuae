A mall unit has to make its case from the concourse, and this one does it by going dark. The shopfront is a full bay of expanded metal mesh with the wordmark and shield backlit through it, so the brand reads as light behind material rather than a sign applied to a wall.

The entrance is a tunnel. Lines of blue neon run the walls, soffit and floor of the threshold in receding frames, pulling the eye through to a reception desk that sits well back from the mall line. It gives the unit a depth the lease plan does not, and it means members arrive already inside the gym's own light rather than the mall's.

Reception carries the same mesh as the shopfront, wrapped around the counter and repeated in a suspended box overhead that washes warm light down onto the till and retail display. Beyond it the fitout stops dressing the building: ducts, services and structure stay exposed over polished concrete, and the training floor runs out under them.

Complete interior fitout — the shopfront and signage, the neon threshold, reception joinery and retail display, MEP coordination through the open ceiling, flooring and the training floor throughout.
