The arch is the whole scheme, used twice. Along the dining wall it is cut as a plaster niche and lit on its inner edge, set against oxblood so the banquette run reads as a series of framed seats rather than one long bench. Behind the counter the same profile is built in timber and shelved, turning product display and menu boards into part of the architecture.

Complete interior fitout: microcement wall finishes throughout, the arched joinery and niche lighting, the service counter and display, and the full loose furniture package.
