The arch carries over from the Sheikh Zayed Road branch, but here it is structural rather than set into a wall. Marble-clad columns split near the ceiling into ribs that run out across the soffit, each rib traced with a continuous line of light, so the end of the hall reads as a lit vault.

Above the dining floor the services are left open — ducts, sprinkler runs and track heads on show — with timber baffles and fluted panels breaking the span into bays. It keeps the ceiling height that a hall this size needs while the arches do the decorative work at eye level.

The floor is set out for volume rather than long sittings: cane-backed chairs and brass-edged marble tables, blue banquette runs along the walls, terrazzo underfoot, and a brass display run at the counter end for the sweets.

Complete interior fitout — MEP coordination through the open ceiling, the arch and vault joinery, terrazzo and marble finishes, counters, and the full furniture package.
