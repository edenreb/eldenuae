The floor is planned around waiting. A curved banquette wraps a planted island at the centre of the lounge, with loose chairs and low tables filling out the rest and a coffee counter along one side, so the room holds a queue without feeling like one.

Meetings happen at the perimeter. Private rooms sit behind black-framed glazed partitions with a frosted band at seated height, each numbered on the glass, which keeps the daylight and the view coming through to the waiting area while the rooms themselves stay private.

Fitout and MEP works throughout — partitions, ceilings and lighting, reception and lounge joinery, and the loose furniture package.
