The plan keeps the glazing public. Cellular offices and meeting rooms sit in black-framed glass with a frosted manifestation band, so the daylight and the view across to the Palm carry into the open-plan floor rather than stopping at a partition. Benching runs beneath suspended linear fittings, with felt screens setting the desk divisions.

Complete interior fitout: the glazed partition system, reception joinery and signage wall, ceiling and lighting, carpet and loose furniture throughout.
