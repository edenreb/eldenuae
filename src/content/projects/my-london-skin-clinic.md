The unit trades on being seen. The shopfront is glazed end to end with no solid return, so the whole reception reads from the promenade — twin curved pods in white solid surface, a strand of pendant lighting between them, and the gold-lit wordmark carried on the fascia above.

Behind reception the palette tightens to one colour. Walls, ceiling and floor run high-gloss white, lit by vertical slots dropped into the ceiling and a continuous skirting wash, so the corridor throws almost no shadow — the finish a treatment floor wants, and the reason the room numbering and the frosted green doors are the only things that register along it.

Fitout and MEP works throughout: the shopfront and fascia signage, reception joinery, the partitioning and door sets to each treatment room, ceilings and lighting, and the mechanical and electrical services serving the clinical rooms.
