The brief was to keep the building's industrial bones on show rather than dress over them — exposed steel trusses, concrete columns, and services left visible as part of the finish. We built the floor plan around a strength zone, a free-weight run, and a dedicated conditioning bay, all lit for a gym that trains members through the Dubai heat and into the evening.

Turnkey scope covered structural steel for the mezzanine, all MEP coordination for ventilation and equipment power runs, and rubber flooring specified for load and acoustic performance.
