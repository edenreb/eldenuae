The brief covered the building rather than a floor of it. Ground and first-floor public areas, all 310 guestrooms, the gym, the spa, the kitchens and the pool bar out to the cabanas — the whole hotel, not the lobby it is usually reduced to.

The property was not trading when we took possession, and that is what made the programme possible. With no guests to work around, trades ran continuously across every floor rather than phasing around occupancy and moving front of house between temporary positions. From the date of mockup approval to handover took six months.

Joinery and FF&E were made rather than specified out. Sofas, armchairs, tables and nightstands were all produced to the scheme, so the guestroom package and the public-area furniture came off the same shop drawings.
