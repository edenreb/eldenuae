A commercial podium shell with a low floor-to-ceiling height and tight service risers, reworked into a full training floor without losing headroom over the free-weight platforms. Signage, branding, and reception joinery were coordinated with the operator's design language from the first design pass.

Delivered as a single turnkey contract from concept design through to opening-day handover.
