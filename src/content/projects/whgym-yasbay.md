Two elements carry the space. The entrance runs on a panoramic glass slider, slow enough on its travel that members stop and shoot themselves against the opening — the door became the gym's photograph before the fitout was finished. Above the floor, a spiral polycarbonate slide turns the drop between levels into part of the circuit rather than a stairwell.

Complete interior fitout: the full training floor, the free-weight and conditioning zones, and the feature lighting run over the turf lanes.
