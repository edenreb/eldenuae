At Elden, employee welfare starts with their safety.

As a specialist in interior fit-out, Elden undertakes projects across retail, hospitality, leisure, commercial and restaurants. To maintain an accident-free work environment, arrangements are made continuously to run safety inductions and training programmes across all sites.

Safety is our utmost priority, and we conduct inductions to make sure the necessary protocols are met on every site.

## What the programmes cover

- Effective communication on site
- Enforcing the use of PPE
- Implementing safety protocols
- Thorough documentation
- Mock fire drills

Every worker on an Elden site is inducted before they start, and the training repeats as crews and site conditions change over the course of a fitout.
